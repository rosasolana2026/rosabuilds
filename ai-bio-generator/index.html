How to write a great professional bio

  1. Lead with what you do, not your title. "I build AI tools for indie founders" is stronger than "Founder & CEO." Start with the value, not the label.
  2. Include a credibility signal. A number, a recognisable company, a specific outcome. One concrete detail beats five vague adjectives.
  3. Match the platform. LinkedIn rewards measured polish. A speaker bio needs momentum. A Twitter bio rewards personality. The same person, three different bios.
  4. Write in third person for bios, first person for social. Third person is convention for formal bios (speaker pages, about pages); first person feels more natural on LinkedIn or Instagram.
  5. End with something forward-looking. Where you're headed, what you're working on, or what you want to be known for. It gives the reader a reason to care.
  6. Refresh it quarterly. Your bio should reflect your current work, not where you were 18 months ago.
